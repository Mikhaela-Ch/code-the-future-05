Greek Mythology

Fun Facts

Curious about Greek mythology? Here are some more interesting facts!

  • There Is No Single Text Covering Greek Mythology: Unlike many other mythologies, there is no single text that contains all of the Greek myths.

    • ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • Greek Mythology Has Origins in The Minoan Civilization: Its origins can be traced back to the Minoan civilization of Crete, which flourished between 3000 and 1100 BC.

    • ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  • It All Begins with Chaos: According to Greek mythology, the universe began as a void called Chaos.
