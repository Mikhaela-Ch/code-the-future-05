Greek Mythology

What is Greek
Mythology?

Greek mythology is the wonderful
collection of myths and legends
from ancient Greece that explain the
origins of the world, the lives
of gods and heroes, and the customs
of the ancient Greeks.

Fun Facts

Here are some interesting facts about Greek mythology!

  1. There Is No Single Text Covering Greek Mythology.
  2. Many of the myths were passed down orally before being written down.
  3. The ancient Greeks believed that their gods and goddesses had human-like qualities and emotions.
  4. Greek mythology has influenced art, literature, and culture for centuries.
  5. Many of the stories in Greek mythology have been adapted into movies, TV shows, and books.
